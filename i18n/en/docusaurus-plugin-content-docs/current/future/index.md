> *"The future of finance is not banks becoming more technological. It is technology becoming finance: invisible, frictionless, present at the right place and time."*

The last part answers the question "what next". Chapter 14 analyzes BNPL and embedded finance. Chapter 15 looks at AI across the loan lifecycle and the limits AI cannot yet overcome. Chapter 16 distills the design principles that stay true as technology changes, drawn from the incidents analyzed throughout the book.

| Chapter | Main topics |
|---|---|
| [Chapter 14. BNPL and embedded finance](/docs/future/ch14-embedded-finance-bnpl) | BNPL mechanics, merchant cash flows, open banking |
| [Chapter 15. AI and the future of lending](/docs/future/ch15-ai-future-of-lending) | AI across the lifecycle, LLMs, generative AI, AI agents, AI-ready infrastructure |
| [Chapter 16. Timeless design principles](/docs/future/ch16-timeless-principles) | Lessons from incidents, principles for financial systems |
