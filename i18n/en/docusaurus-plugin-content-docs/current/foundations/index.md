> *"Before building a system, understand why the system needs to exist."*

This part answers the question "why". Before discussing microservices or databases, readers need to understand why credit exists, why a lender can never be sure who will repay, and why a consumer loan costs so much more than a mortgage. That is the subject of Chapter 1.

Chapter 2 turns those economic ideas into numbers: the annuity formula, amortization schedules, measuring the "true" rate with IRR, APR and EIR, expected-loss models, and the reason every serious financial system forbids floating-point numbers for money.

Ms. Lan's 18,500,000 VND loan, the running example of the book, first appears here.
