## The true cost of credit

The nominal rate excludes fees. With an up-front fee (deducted from the disbursement) and a fixed monthly fee, the true cost is measured by the IRR of the cash the borrower actually receives and pays, annualized as APR or EIR.

## Early settlement {/* #early-settlement */}

To repay a loan before its term, the borrower pays the outstanding principal, the interest accrued since the last paid instalment, and a prepayment fee if the contract has one. This calculator gives the settlement amount and compares it with keeping to the schedule, as discussed in [Chapter 5, section 5.3](/docs/products/ch05-revenue-portfolio#rủi-ro-trả-nợ-trước-hạn).
