> *"Data is the raw material. The model is the recipe. Knowing when the model is right and when it is wrong is the real competitive advantage."*

This part answers the question "how to be smarter". A lending platform that runs correctly is only the starting point. To compete and survive, a lender must decide well who gets credit (Chapter 11) and at what price (Chapter 12). Chapter 13 looks at the people behind those decisions: borrowers are not perfectly rational, and a responsible lending system has to be designed with that in mind.
