> *"Understand the product before building the system. The best system serving the wrong product is still a failure."*

This part answers the question "what". A loan is not just an interest rate: it combines a product type (instalment, revolving line, buy now pay later), a fee schedule, promotional rules and many decisions about customer experience.

Chapter 3 introduces product taxonomy and the tariff system that lets one platform serve dozens of products without rewriting code. Chapter 4 follows the customer from application to settlement. Chapter 5 takes the lender's view: where revenue comes from and how a loan portfolio is managed over time.
